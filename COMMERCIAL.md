## Needs a commercial license

- Using units, firmware, the desktop applications or the design files on paid work by anyone whose revenue is US$100,000 or more: a production company, a rental house, a broadcaster, a post-production facility.
- Selling, renting, leasing or hiring out units or kits built from these files, with or without modification. This applies to small users too.
- Making boards or firmware for sale, or including them in a product or service.
- Offering the software or designs as part of a paid service, for example timecode rental or a sync service built on the applications.

If you are unsure whether your use counts, ask before relying on it.

Contact the author through the GitHub profile https://github.com/idnawsi, or open an issue in this repository asking for commercial terms. Terms (one-time, per unit, or per year) are agreed individually and are not published here.
