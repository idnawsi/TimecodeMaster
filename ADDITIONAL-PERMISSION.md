A **Small User** is an individual, or a business or other organisation, whose gross revenue from all business and professional activity was less than US$100,000 in its most recent full year (calendar or fiscal). A Small User may use the software, firmware, desktop applications and design files in this repository (the "Software") on its own paid work: photography, video, film, audio recording, live events and similar creative work done for clients or for sale. 1. Sell, rent, lease or hire out units, kits, boards, firmware, software or copies of them, with or without changes.
2. Make units or boards for other people, or for sale.
3. Offer the Software or units to others as a product or a paid service, for example timecode rental, or a sync service built on the applications.
4. Include the Software in a product or service sold to others.

A rental house, a production company, a broadcaster or any other business with revenue of US$100,000 or more is not a Small User. It needs a commercial license to use the Software on paid work.

If the revenue of a Small User reaches US$100,000, this permission continues to the end of that year. Work already started may be finished. After that a commercial license is needed.
